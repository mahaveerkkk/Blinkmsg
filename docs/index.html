Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlinkMsg ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user data. Our core philosophy is simple: We do not collect, store, sell, or monetize any of your personal data. BlinkMsg is engineered specifically to eliminate data retention entirely.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect your real name, phone number, email address, contacts, or social media links.
  • No Identity Profiles: User profiles are stored locally on your device in volatile sandbox storage and can be completely purged at any time.
  • No Message Logs: Messages are ephemeral. Once delivered and read (or expired via timer), they are permanently incinerated from memory.

2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

BlinkMsg requests minimal device permissions strictly necessary for user-initiated cryptographic features:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively within the app to scan BlinkMsg QR codes for adding friends or joining rooms. The camera video feed is processed 100% locally on your device; no video or image frames are ever transmitted, recorded, or stored remotely.
  • Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively when you press and hold the voice recorder button to record listen-once ephemeral audio notes. Voice notes are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before transport and are permanently vaporized upon listening.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly for tactile sensory feedback upon sending, receiving, or burning messages.

3. End-to-End Encryption & Security

All communication channels in BlinkMsg utilize client-side AES-256-GCM hardware cryptographic primitives. Room encryption keys are negotiated directly between peer devices and are never transmitted to or readable by any relay server.

4. Data Retention & One-Tap Emergency Wipe

BlinkMsg stores messages strictly in temporary, encrypted local storage. Users can trigger an instant "Emergency Wipe All Data" button in Settings at any time, which irrevocably purges all encryption keys, contacts, cached media, and conversation history from the device.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses encrypted websocket relay infrastructure strictly to route blinded ciphertext packets between active peers. No analytics trackers, behavioral profiling SDKs, or third-party advertising frameworks are integrated into BlinkMsg.

6. Children's Privacy

BlinkMsg does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any user, no children's data is retained.
